Department of Electronics Engineering

Mastering modern electronics, microprocessors, communication networks, embedded systems, and VLSI circuit design.

About the Department

The Electronics Engineering branch at Government Polytechnic Chopan is designed to prepare students for the rapidly growing electronics, communication, and automation sectors. The program emphasizes the principles of electronics, circuit design, and signal processing, while integrating advancements in modern communication systems and industrial technologies.

Curriculum Highlights

The structured curriculum provides comprehensive coverage of key domains:

Solid-State Electronics: Electronic Devices & Circuits
Digital Architecture: Digital Electronics & Microprocessors (8085/8086)
Telecommunication: Analog & Digital Communication Systems
Industrial Drives: Industrial & Power Electronics
Smart Systems: Embedded Systems & Microcontroller Programming
Sensors & Controls: Electronic Instruments and Measurements
Emerging Domains: IoT, VLSI Basics & Renewable Energy Electronics

Practical learning is central to the department’s training philosophy. Students develop strong technical skills through laboratory experiments, circuit simulation tools, and hardware fabrication projects.

Career Opportunities: Graduates can pursue rewarding careers in telecom, hardware design, embedded systems, industrial automation, robotics, consumer electronics, and government sectors, or choose higher education in advanced engineering fields.

State-of-the-Art Labs

Department Laboratories

Digital Electronics Laboratory

The Digital Electronics Laboratory provides hands-on exposure to logic gates, combinational and sequential circuit designs, flip-flops, multiplexers, and counter circuits. Equipped with regulated power supplies, IC testers, digital trainer kits, and oscilloscopes.

Electronics Devices & Circuits Lab

Dedicated to fundamental component testing including semiconductor diodes, transistors (BJT, FET, MOSFET), operational amplifiers, signal generators, and communication modulation circuits with high-precision measurement instruments.
