Central Engineering Workshops

Hands-on fabrication, industrial machinery, metalworking, carpentry, welding, and practical trade mastery.

Practical Industrial Training

The Central Workshops at Government Polytechnic Chopan form the backbone of engineering craftsmanship and practical technical skill building. Every engineering diploma student undergoes comprehensive hands-on training across foundational engineering trades.

Workshop Sections & Trades

Machine & Turning Shop: Center lathes, shaper machines, drill presses & bench grinders
Fitting & Benchwork: Precision hand fitting, filing, marking, tapping & die work
Carpentry & Pattern Making: Wood planning, mortise & tenon joints, pattern fabrication
Welding & Sheet Metal: Arc welding, gas welding, spot welding & sheet shearing
Shop Floor Infrastructure

Workshop Facilities

Machining & Turning Section

Equipped with industrial-grade turning lathes, milling attachments, shaping tools, and precision tooling gauges for manufacturing mechanical test specimens.

Fitting, Carpentry & Fabrication Section

Equipped with heavy-duty bench vises, surface plates, vernier height gauges, oxy-acetylene gas welding stations, and electric arc welding apparatus.
