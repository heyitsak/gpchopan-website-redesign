Applied Sciences & Humanities

The foundational pillar of diploma engineering: Engineering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics & Communication Skills.

Academic Foundation

The Department of Applied Sciences and Humanities plays a vital foundational role across all diploma engineering programs at Government Polytechnic Chopan. It equips first-year students with scientific inquiry, analytical problem solving, and professional communication skills necessary for advanced engineering studies.

Core Disciplines

Applied Physics: Mechanics, Optics, Modern Physics, Quantum & Thermodynamics
Applied Chemistry: Material Science, Electrochemistry, Polymers & Water Treatment
Applied Mathematics: Algebra, Trigonometry, Calculus & Differential Equations
Communication Skills: Technical Writing, Verbal Fluency & Interview Etiquette
Scientific Laboratories

Department Laboratories

Applied Physics & Chemistry Laboratory

Equipped with optical spectrometers, laser apparatus, compound microscopes, titration units, and chemical analysis tools for empirical scientific validation.

Language & Communication Skills Lab

Dedicated digital language lab with interactive audio headsets, speech modulation software, and presentation facilities to build student confidence and workplace communication proficiency.
