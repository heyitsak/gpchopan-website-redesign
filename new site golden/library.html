LIBRARY

Facility: Central Library

The college library serves as a rich source of knowledge and plays an important role in supporting the academic needs of diploma students. It is well-stocked with textbooks, reference books related to various branches of engineering and applied sciences. The library provides a quiet and disciplined environment, encouraging students to develop the habit of self-study and broaden their understanding beyond the classroom. With its organized collection and comfortable seating, the library helps students enhance their learning experience and achieve academic excellence.
