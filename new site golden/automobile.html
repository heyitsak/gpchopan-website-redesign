Department of Automobile Engineering

Pioneering vehicle mechanics, transmission systems, automotive electronics, thermal engineering, and electric mobility.

About the Department

The Automobile Engineering branch at Government Polytechnic Chopan provides specialized education in the design, manufacturing, operation, and maintenance of automotive vehicles. The course blends core mechanical principles with cutting-edge automotive technology, preparing students to meet the evolving demands of the transportation industry.

Curriculum Highlights

The specialized curriculum encompasses comprehensive automotive disciplines:

Powerplants: Internal Combustion (IC) Engines (Petrol & Diesel)
Structure & Frame: Automotive Chassis, Body & Suspension Design
Drive Systems: Transmission Systems (Manual, Automatic & EV)
Vehicle Electronics: Auto Electrical and Electronic Systems
Fluid Power: Hydraulics and Pneumatics in Modern Vehicles
Diagnosis & Service: Vehicle Maintenance, Testing & Emission Control
Next-Gen Mobility: Electric Vehicles (EV) & Hybrid Technology

Practical training is a major strength of the department. Students gain hands-on expertise in automotive workshops, engine testing labs, and computer-aided design (CAD) setups.

Career Opportunities: Graduates can pursue careers in automotive manufacturing, vehicle testing, service engineering, fleet maintenance, and component design, or pursue higher studies and entrepreneurship in automotive services.

Heavy & Thermal Testing

Department Laboratories

Thermal Engineering & Hydraulics Lab

Features internal combustion engine cut-section models, multi-cylinder petrol and diesel engine test rigs, dynamometers, hydraulic turbines, and fluid flow measurement equipment.

Mechanics & Material Testing Lab

Equipped with Universal Testing Machines (UTM), impact testers, hardness testing apparatus, torsion testing rigs, and chassis dynamometers for comprehensive automotive structural evaluation.
