WORKSHOPS

The Workshop at Government Polytechnic Chopan provides hands-on training in various trades such as fitting, welding, machining, carpentry, and sheet metal work. Equipped with modern tools and machinery, it helps students develop essential practical skills, safety awareness, and craftsmanship. The workshop bridges the gap between theoretical learning and real-world engineering applications, fostering technical competence and innovation among students.

SHOPS

The college workshop is a vital facility where students gain hands-on experience with basic engineering tools, machines, and processes. It provides a safe and organized environment to practice practical skills and apply theoretical knowledge learned in the classroom.

The workshop is divided into different sections equipped with tools and machines for exercises such as cutting, shaping, joining, and assembling materials. These activities help students develop technical skills, precision, and problem-solving abilities essential for any engineering discipline.

Students also get practical exposure to general engineering practices, including measurements, material testing, and simple mechanical and electrical exercises. This ensures that they understand the real-world applications of concepts studied in their courses.
