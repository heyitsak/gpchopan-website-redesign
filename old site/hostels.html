BOYS HOSTEL

The boys hostel offers convenient and affordable accommodation for students coming from different places. It provides well-furnished rooms, a study-friendly atmosphere, regular mess facilities, and recreational space for relaxation. The hostel emphasizes cleanliness, discipline, and cooperation among students, making it a supportive place to live and learn.

GIRLS HOSTEL

The college provides a well-maintained girls hostel within the campus to ensure a safe and comfortable stay for female students. The hostel is equipped with spacious rooms, proper ventilation, clean drinking water, and hygienic mess facilities. Special care is taken to maintain discipline, security, and a homely environment so that students can focus on their studies with peace of mind.
